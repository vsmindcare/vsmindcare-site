Privacy & Patient Rights

Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective August 24, 2026

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can access this information. Please review it carefully.

Your information. Your rights. Our responsibilities.

VS MindCare is required by law to maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information, provide you with this notice, and follow the privacy practices described in it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Obtain a paper or electronic copy of your medical record.
  • Ask us to correct information you believe is incorrect or incomplete.
  • Request confidential communications by a particular method or at a particular location.
  • Ask us to limit how we use or share certain information.
  • Receive a list of certain disclosures of your health information.
  • Obtain a paper copy of this Notice of Privacy Practices.
  • Choose someone to act on your behalf.
  • File a complaint if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

Some requests may be subject to legal limitations. We will explain our decision if we cannot fulfill a request.

Your choices

For certain types of information, you may tell us how you want it shared, including sharing information with family members, friends, or others involved in your care.

We will obtain your written authorization before using or disclosing your information when authorization is required by law, including for most marketing purposes, the sale of protected health information, and certain uses or disclosures of psychotherapy notes. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent we have already relied upon it.

How we may use and disclose your information

Treatment

We may use your information to provide, coordinate, or manage your care and communicate with other healthcare professionals involved in your treatment.

Payment

We may use and share your information to bill health plans or other responsible parties and receive payment for services.

Healthcare operations

We may use your information to operate VS MindCare, improve services, conduct quality reviews, and contact you when necessary.

Legal and public-interest purposes

We may use or disclose information when permitted or required by law, including for public health and safety, health oversight, workers’ compensation, law enforcement, judicial or administrative proceedings, and to prevent or reduce a serious and imminent threat to health or safety.

Mental-health and substance-use information

Certain mental-health, psychotherapy, and substance-use-disorder records may receive additional protection under federal or California law. VS MindCare will obtain additional authorization before using or disclosing these records when required.

Electronic communications and telehealth

VS MindCare may communicate with you through approved electronic systems, including secure patient portals, telehealth platforms, email, telephone, or text messaging, according to your preferences and applicable law.

Ordinary email and text messaging may carry privacy risks. Please do not use ordinary email or website forms to send urgent concerns or detailed medical information.

Our responsibilities

  • We are required by law to maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information.
  • We will notify you promptly if a breach may have compromised the privacy or security of your information.
  • We must follow the duties and privacy practices described in this notice and provide you with a copy upon request.
  • We will not use or share your information other than as described here unless you authorize it in writing or the law permits or requires it.

Changes to this notice

We may change this notice and apply the revised notice to information we already maintain and information we receive in the future. The current notice will be available at our office and on our website.

Questions or complaints

You may contact VS MindCare if you have questions, want to exercise your privacy rights, or wish to file a complaint. VS MindCare will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint or exercising your rights.
