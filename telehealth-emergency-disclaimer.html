Telehealth & Safety

Telehealth & Emergency Disclaimer

Effective August 24, 2026

The VS MindCare website, email, fax, contact forms, and appointment-request systems are not monitored continuously and must not be used for emergencies or urgent medical concerns.

Not for emergencies

If you are experiencing a medical or psychiatric emergency, call 911, go to the nearest emergency department, or call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Do not wait for a response from VS MindCare before obtaining emergency assistance.

Telehealth services

VS MindCare provides psychiatric services through telehealth when clinically appropriate. Telehealth uses electronic communications to allow a patient and healthcare provider to meet from separate locations.

Patients must be physically located in California at the time of every telehealth appointment. You may be asked to confirm your current physical location at the beginning of each visit.

Telehealth may not be appropriate for every person, condition, or situation. VS MindCare may recommend in-person evaluation, a higher level of care, emergency treatment, or referral to another provider when clinically necessary.

Limitations and risks

Potential limitations and risks include technology or Internet failures, interrupted communication, limits on physical examination, privacy risks associated with electronic communications, and the possibility that telehealth may be insufficient for a particular clinical concern. In-person care, laboratory testing, vital signs, or other evaluation may be required.

VS MindCare uses reasonable safeguards and approved technology to protect patient privacy. No electronic communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure or uninterrupted.

Patient responsibilities

For telehealth appointments, patients are responsible for:

  • Being physically located in California.
  • Providing an accurate current location and emergency contact information.
  • Participating from a reasonably private and safe environment.
  • Using a reliable Internet connection and compatible device.
  • Not driving or operating machinery during an appointment.
  • Informing the provider if another person is present.
  • Seeking emergency help when instructed or when immediate safety concerns arise.

Prescriptions

Submitting an appointment request or completing an evaluation does not guarantee that medication will be prescribed. Medication decisions, including those involving controlled substances, are based on clinical appropriateness, patient safety, professional judgment, and applicable federal and California requirements. Additional records, testing, monitoring, identification, or in-person evaluation may be required.

Website information is not medical advice

Information on this website is provided for general educational purposes. It is not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, medical advice, or a substitute for an evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.

Viewing this website, sending an email, submitting a contact form, or requesting an appointment does not establish a provider-patient relationship. A provider-patient relationship is established only after VS MindCare formally accepts you as a patient and completes the required consent and intake process.

Communication response times

General website and insurance inquiries are typically answered within 24 hours, but response times are not guaranteed. Do not send urgent concerns, medication emergencies, detailed medical information, or crisis messages through ordinary email or general website forms.
