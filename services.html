Services

Comprehensive psychiatric care, one steady step at a time.

From your first evaluation to ongoing treatment, every step is built around your history, goals, and comfort.

Step One

Comprehensive Psychiatric Evaluation

An in-depth 60-minute initial assessment covering your history, symptoms, and goals — plus collaborative treatment planning and evidence-based diagnostic tools.

Step Two

Medication Management

Careful selection and monitoring of medications to optimize effectiveness, with adjustments as needed and clear education about benefits, side effects, and expectations.

Step Three

Ongoing Mental Health Treatment

Regular follow-up appointments to monitor progress, supportive discussions about coping skills, and coordination with therapists or other providers when needed.

Throughout

Supportive & Holistic Care

Psychoeducation about your diagnosis, lifestyle strategies for sleep and stress, and encouragement toward therapy or mindfulness when it would help.

Conditions We Treat

A wide range of mental health conditions.

Depression Anxiety Disorders ADHD (inattentive, hyperactive, combined) Bipolar Disorder Schizophrenia & Psychotic Disorders Adjustment Disorders Insomnia & Sleep Difficulties PTSD & Trauma-Related Conditions Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD) And more
