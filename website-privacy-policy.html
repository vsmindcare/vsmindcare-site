Website Privacy

Website Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026

VS MindCare respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to the public VS MindCare website. It does not replace our Notice of Privacy, which explains how we use and disclose protected health information in connection with healthcare services.

Information you provide

You may voluntarily provide information when you submit an insurance or contact form, send us an email, call or fax our office, or schedule through a third-party platform. This may include your name, email address, phone number, insurance provider, preferred contact method, and other information you choose to provide.

Please do not submit symptoms, diagnoses, medications, treatment information, or other sensitive medical information through ordinary email or general website forms.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, our hosting provider and other website-service providers may automatically receive technical information such as your Internet Protocol address, browser and device type, operating system, referring website, pages visited, access date and time, and diagnostic, security, or performance information.

VS MindCare does not currently use advertising pixels or behavioral advertising tools on this website.

How we use information

  • Respond to general questions.
  • Contact you about insurance or appointment availability.
  • Direct you to an appropriate scheduling platform.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, or security problems.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting a website form does not establish a provider-patient relationship.

Google Forms

The website may use Google Forms to collect general contact and insurance information. Responses are maintained within VS MindCare’s Google Workspace environment. Google may process technical information according to its own terms and privacy practices. Please do not include medical information in a general website form unless it specifically states that it is intended for that purpose.

Appointment scheduling

Appointment links may direct you to Grow Therapy or another healthcare platform. Information submitted through those platforms is governed by the platform’s privacy policy and applicable agreements with VS MindCare. VS MindCare does not control every aspect of an independent third-party platform’s website, security practices, or privacy policy.

Website hosting and external services

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and may use services or resources provided by Google Workspace, Google Forms, Grow Therapy, Google Fonts, and image-hosting or content-delivery providers. These providers may receive technical information necessary to deliver their services.

Cookies and tracking technologies

The website itself does not currently place advertising cookies or use behavioral advertising trackers. Third-party services embedded in or linked from the website may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies. Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies.

Email and text messaging

Ordinary email and text messages may not be fully secure. Do not use them for urgent concerns or detailed medical information. If you are experiencing an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. For mental-health crisis support, call or text 988.

Information sharing

VS MindCare does not sell personal information collected through this website. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the website or respond to requests, when required by law or legal process, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business reorganization or transfer subject to applicable law.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we maintain. However, no website, email system, or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain website inquiries only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, applicable requirements, and dispute resolution. Healthcare records are retained according to applicable medical-record laws and VS MindCare policies.

Children’s privacy

The website is not directed to children under 13, and VS MindCare does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through the public website without appropriate authorization. Parents or legal guardians should contact VS MindCare directly regarding services for a minor.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information submitted through the website, request correction or deletion of a general inquiry when legally permitted, ask questions about this policy, or exercise other rights available under applicable law. Requests involving medical records or protected health information are handled according to our Notice of Privacy and applicable healthcare laws.

External links and policy changes

VS MindCare is not responsible for the content, availability, or privacy practices of third-party websites. We may update this policy periodically. The revised policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
